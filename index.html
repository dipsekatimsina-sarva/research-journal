Loblolly Pine
Biomass
Spatial modeling of biomass allocation in Pinus taeda across the southern U.S. using Dirichlet regression with geographic covariates.

Adding latitude and longitude significantly improved model fit. Foliage proportion increases at higher latitudes — relevant to carbon sequestration policy.
